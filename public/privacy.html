01Who we are.

Robes is run by Robes Technologies Limited, 39 Seapark Road, Clontarf, Dublin D03 R673, Ireland. We are the controller of your data. Write to us any time at hello@byrobes.com.

02What we collect.

  • Your email and name.
  • What you give Robes to style you: your requests, your wardrobe pieces, your photos (including an optional close-up), and your size facts. Never your weight.
  • How you use Robes — the screens you open and the actions you take — kept in Robes’ own database.

You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Apple or Google. Robes never holds a password. Robes is for people aged 18 and over.

04Your location.

Optional, and only to dress you for the weather. Robes rounds your position to about a kilometre and refreshes it from time to time. That rounded position goes to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for your town’s name and to Open-Meteo for the forecast. You can type a town instead, or say no.

05Your digital twin.

Your optional close-up gives Robes your colouring and the reference for a full-length model of you, created by Google Gemini from the close-up and your size facts. Your looks are shown on that model, so they show your face.

Robes never reads your measurements from photos — they come from the facts and sliders you set. Remove your close-up and the model is redrawn without your face. Delete your account and the model goes with it. If you share a look, anyone with its link can see it until you turn the link off.

06Usage counts and screen recordings.

Robes asks before measuring anything with PostHog, and you can change your mind under You › Privacy. Usage counts record screens and taps — never your photos, your words or your email. Errors are recorded so we can fix them. Screen recordings are a separate yes: text, photos and typed answers are hidden, they’re stored in Europe, and only Robes can see them.

07Emails from Robes.

A note when your looks are ready, a note when a receipt has been read, an optional morning cue, and occasional wardrobe nudges. The first three are on unless you turn them off; nudges are off unless you turn them on. Change them under You › Notifications, or use the one-click unsubscribe in every email. No tracking pixels: Robes logs that an email was sent, never whether you opened it.

08Receipts you forward.

Google Gemini reads forwarded order confirmations to find the pieces, which wait in your Inbox until you approve them. The original email is deleted after seven days; its sender and subject are cleared 90 days after you decide.

10Our legal basis.

  • Contract — to run the service you signed up for.
  • Consent — your close-up and model, your location, usage counts, recordings and optional emails. Withdraw it any time in the app.
  • Legitimate interest — error reports, rate limits and server logs.

11Your rights.

You can access, correct, download or delete your data, object to how it’s used, or withdraw consent — in the app or by email. We answer within a month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

12Your choices.

Every photo step can be skipped. Edit or remove any wardrobe piece whenever you like. Under You › Account, download your data, or delete your account and everything in it — including what PostHog holds.

13Questions about your data.

Write to hello@byrobes.com. A person reads every message.
