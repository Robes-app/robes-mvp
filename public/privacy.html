Privacy notice

Plain-English version for the Robes beta. Last updated July 2026.

What we collect

Your email, name and the styling inputs you give us — prompts, wardrobe items, and any photos you upload (including the optional colour and silhouette photos in your style notes).

How it's used

Only to run Robes for you: generating looks and analyses, storing your wardrobe and style profile, and improving the product during beta. Photos are sent to our AI providers (Google Gemini, Anthropic) solely to produce your results, and images are hosted on Cloudinary. Your profile lives in Supabase; waitlist and feedback records live in Airtable. We never sell your data.

Your choices

The colour and silhouette photos are optional — every step that asks for one can be skipped. You can update or remove wardrobe items at any time, and you can request full deletion of your account and data by emailing us.

Contact

Anything about your data: hello@byrobes.com
