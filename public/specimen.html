What to look for: the left cut carries higher stroke contrast and longer, sharper serifs — showier at scale, especially in the italic. The right cut sits closer to a classical Garamond — rounder, quieter, a touch wider in the lowercase. "Restrained not minimal" is the right column's temperament; "a little more Jacquemus" is the left's. Both hold weight 300 at these sizes; below ~17px both get fragile, which is why the app floors its serifs there.
