Privacy notice

Plain-English version for the Robes beta. Last updated September 2026.

What we collect

Your email, name and the styling inputs you give us — prompts, wardrobe items, and any photos you upload (including the optional colour and silhouette photos in your style notes). We also record how you use Robes: the pages you open and the actions you take.

How it's used

Only to run Robes for you: generating looks and analyses, storing your wardrobe and style profile, and improving the product during beta. Photos are sent to our AI providers (Google Gemini, Anthropic) solely to produce your results, and images are hosted on Cloudinary. Your profile lives in Supabase; waitlist and feedback records live in Airtable; how the app is used is measured in PostHog, on European servers. We never sell your data.

Session recordings

So we can see where Robes is confusing, PostHog records sessions in the app — the pages you open, what you click, and what you type, including the prompts you write. Your wardrobe photographs appear in them, because they are on the screen as you work. Passwords are never recorded. Recordings are stored on European servers, are visible only to us, and are never used for advertising.

Emails from Robes

Robes can email you: when the three looks you asked for are ready, the odd note when your wardrobe is ready for more (a look waiting on your model, pieces Robes is borrowing, a week with nothing planned), and, if you switch it on, a morning line on the days you have planned. Every one of these is a choice you make in the app — the looks-ready note and the nudges with one tap on your first day, the morning line under Account details — and every email carries a one-click link to stop it. We send them through Resend. There are no tracking pixels: we record that an email was sent, and whether you opened Robes from its link, nothing more.

Receipts you forward

If you forward an order confirmation to your own Robes address (yours alone, shown under Add a piece → Forward a receipt), Robes reads the email to find the pieces in it — their names, brands, colours, prices, sizes and product photographs — and holds them on your wardrobe page until you look them over. When the receipt has been read, Robes emails you to say so, with a link to the review; that email carries the same one-click stop as every other. Nothing is filed into your wardrobe without you. The reading is done by Google Gemini, the same model that reads your photographs; the email's text is kept only as the pieces it produced, and product images are copied to our image host so they survive the retailer's link expiring. Forward only emails you are happy for Robes to read.

Your choices

The colour and silhouette photos are optional — every step that asks for one can be skipped. You can update or remove wardrobe items at any time, and you can request full deletion of your account and data by emailing us. That includes your session recordings — ask and we will delete them.
