Common questions.

What is a digital twin?

A likeness of you, built from an optional close-up and the size facts you set, so you can see each look on you before you get dressed. It’s built to be accurate, never slimmed or smoothed. Your face is used only to show you your own looks. Remove the close-up and your model is redrawn without your face.

Do I have to make a twin?

No. Start with your pieces instead and see them on a Robes model matched to your size and shape. Every photo step can be skipped.

Do I need to add my whole wardrobe first?

No. Your first looks come from your style notes, as a stylist’s suggestions. As you add pieces, Robes builds looks from what you own.

Why does Robes say I wore something I didn’t?

A planned look counts as worn automatically. If you wore something else, tap Wore something else on that day — or add the look you did wear, even from last week.

Does Robes ask me to buy things?

Rarely. Every look starts with what you own. When a look needs something you don’t have, Robes names the piece and the looks it completes. There’s no shopping in the app.

Will I lose what I’ve added?

No. Everything you add stays exactly as you left it, on iPhone and the web. Sign in with the same email on both.

How do I delete my twin, my photos or my account?

Under You › Account, download your data or delete your account and everything in it. Pieces you remove are deleted after 30 days.
