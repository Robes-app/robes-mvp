Get set up

Three steps. Two minutes.

  1. 01

    Get TestFlight

    Apple’s free app for trying apps before they launch. Get TestFlight on the App Store

  2. 02

    Join the beta

    Open the Robes invite. Tap Accept, then Install.

  3. 03

    Sign in

    Enter your email and we’ll send you a code.

Before you start

Have a few things to hand.

A few of your favourite pieces, a look you’ve saved and love, and your diary for the next fortnight.

Things to try

Six things. Any order.

Take your time. There’s no right way to do this. When you’re ready, tell us how it went.

  1. 01

    Choose who Robes styles

    Pick a Robes model, or make one that looks like you.

    Stuck?

    Tap your profile in the top left, then add your digital twin.

  2. 02

    Add your favourite pieces

    Add a few pieces you love, so Robes can create looks with what you already own.

    Stuck?

    Open Wardrobe in the main menu, and add as many pieces as you like.

  3. 03

    Tell Robes your style

    What you love, what works for you, and what you’d never wear.

    Stuck?

    Tap your profile in the top left, then update your Style DNA.

  4. 04

    Recreate a look you love

    Saved a look on Instagram or Pinterest, or spotted one in a magazine? Capture it, and Robes will recreate it with your own clothes.

    Stuck?

    Start a new look and try the Capture button.

  5. 05

    Dress for your Christmas party

    Sequins, satin or a sharp suit? Tell Robes about the night and see what it pulls together from your wardrobe.

    Stuck?

    Start a new look and try the Create button.

  6. DecemberDiary
    1. Fri5Office drinks
    2. Sat13Christmas party
    3. Thu18Lunch with Ciara
    4. Sat20Dinner, 8pm
    06

    Plan your festive fortnight

    Map out what you’ll wear to the next few things in your diary.

    Stuck?

    Open Diary in the main menu to plan what you’ll wear.
