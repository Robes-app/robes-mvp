05

Voice & register

Robes speaks as a stylist with a point of view — warm, specific, never machine, never salesy.

  • Never "AI". Not in product copy, not in marketing, not in error states. Robes reads, files, composes, dresses — verbs of hands, not processors. ("Generating…" is equally banned.)
  • Errors own the failure warmly. "Robes couldn't finish those looks — please try again in a moment." Never raw server text, never blame, never a schema leaking into her ear.
  • Banned constructions (enforced on generated copy, held by hand in product copy): effortlessly · elevated · versatile · perfect for · ensuring · "X yet Y" — and no exclamation marks, anywhere.
  • Honesty over promise. Below 15 pieces the copy says looks are woven from what she owns plus editorial fills — it never claims closet-only styling it can't deliver. Her decisions are named as hers: a free day reads "Left free.", never "Nothing yet."
  • The italic is the wink. The serif's italic second line carries the charm — "worn three ways." · "dressed for anything." — so the sans stays plain and working.
  • Her words win. Wherever her typed plan and a rewrite compete for a title, her words surface.
