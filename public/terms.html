Terms of use

Robes is in beta — these terms are short on purpose. Last updated July 2026.

What Robes is

Robes is an AI styling service. You share pieces, photos and preferences; Robes generates styling suggestions, imagery and wardrobe notes. Suggestions are creative guidance, not professional advice — trust your own eye first.

Your account

Keep your login details to yourself and use Robes only for your personal styling. You must be at least 16 to create an account. You can stop using Robes at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing us.

Your content

Photos and text you upload stay yours. You give us permission to process them so we can generate your looks, analyses and boards. Don't upload images you don't have the right to share.

Beta expectations

Robes is under active development. Features may change, images occasionally fail to generate, and AI output can be imperfect. We'd genuinely love to hear when something feels off.
